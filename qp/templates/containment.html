Two separate protections

Detection is best-effort; containment is the boundary that holds even if detection misses.

{{ icon('search') }}1. Prompt-injection detection (best-effort)

Pattern scanner + AI classifier over every uploaded document, plus checks on the generated code. Hits become review items. Upload the poisoned sample quote to see it flagged on the timeline.

{{ icon('box') }}2. Sandbox containment (the real boundary)

Even if injected text fools the AI into writing hostile code, that code runs with no network, no secrets, a read-only filesystem, a non-root user, no capabilities, process/memory/time limits, and the container is destroyed. The test results above are the evidence.
